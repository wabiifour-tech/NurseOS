About NurseOS

A New Operating System for Nursing Care

NurseOS is a comprehensive healthcare technology platform built by a nurse who understood the gaps in clinical workflows. It integrates AI-powered clinical decision support, care coordination, nurse credentialing, analytics, and education into one unified system — designed specifically for the realities of nursing practice.
